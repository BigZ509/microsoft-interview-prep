## 3. Independent problem-solving

My biggest weakness is identifying the correct approach when the problem does not explicitly disclose its pattern.

Build exercises that train me to:

- Recognize relevant characteristics of a problem.
- Identify an appropriate data structure.
- Develop a brute-force solution.
- Identify its bottlenecks.
- Derive an optimized solution.
- Explain time and space complexity.
- Test edge cases.
- Respond to interviewer hints.

Include daily blind coding assessments where the pattern, difficulty and solution are hidden.

Prioritize understanding over memorization and independent solving over increasing the solved-problem count.

## 4. Keep OOP focused

- Classes and objects
- Constructors and self
- Instance attributes and methods
- Inheritance and super()
- Composition
- Object references
- Managing collections of objects
- Validation
- Cloning and copying objects

Create a small, progressive challenge bank:

- Phone book
- Bank account
- Library management system
- Parking lot
- Simplified Canva implementation

Do not expand this into a full software architecture or design patterns course.
